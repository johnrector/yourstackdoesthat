## The need
A list that reflects what you actually agreed to do, in mail and meetings and messages, not just what you remembered to type.

## What you used to buy
A task manager with a paid tier, and the hour a week spent grooming it.

## Your stack does that
It adds what you committed to as it sees you commit, marks things done when it sees them done, and each morning shows the three that matter today.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to mail, messages, and the calendar. A scheduled task in the evening that reconciles the list against the day. Memory, or a plain file, holds the list. The list is a record and it should be yours, in a form you can open without anyone's permission. A text file in a folder qualifies.
