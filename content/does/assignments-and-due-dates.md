## The need
Six classes, forty deadlines, and the one that moved.

## What you used to buy
A student planner app, paid, that you had to keep updated by hand.

## Your stack does that
It reads the syllabi and the announcements, keeps the deadlines on the calendar, and each Sunday says what is due this week and what to start now.

## How, in plain terms
The syllabi in a folder and a connector to the calendar and to mail. Some learning systems offer a connector or a calendar export; use it where it exists. A scheduled task on Sunday evening. The grades live in the school's system, which is a record. The plan is yours.
