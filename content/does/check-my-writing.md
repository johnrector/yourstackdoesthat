## The need
Every email, every post, every proposal read once by someone who catches what you missed and does not change your voice.

## What you used to buy
A grammar checker with a monthly charge, a browser extension, and an upsell.

## Your stack does that
It reads the draft the way you told it to, once, and gives it back with the errors fixed and the voice untouched.

## How, in plain terms
A skill: a page that says what to fix, what to leave alone, and the words you never use. That is the entire product. It runs on everything you send. This is the clearest case on the list, and the reason the clearest company in that category renamed itself.
