## The need
Thirty sources, one citation style, and a bibliography that has to be right.

## What you used to buy
A citation manager with a paid tier for storage and syncing.

## Your stack does that
It reads the folder of sources, keeps the citation for each, and formats the bibliography in the style the course requires.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the folder where the PDFs live. A skill for the citation style and the course's quirks. The library of sources is a record; keep it in a folder that is yours and back it up. The manager in front of it was a product.
