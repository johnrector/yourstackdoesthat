## The need
Hours, prices, parking, whether you do the thing, whether you can do it by Friday. The same twenty questions, every week.

## What you used to buy
A chat widget subscription for the website, or an answering service.

## Your stack does that
It reads the mailbox and the messages, answers the twenty questions from your page, and passes the rest to you with a suggested reply.

## How, in plain terms
A skill: one page of the questions and your exact answers. A connector to the mailbox and messaging. A scheduled task that runs every few minutes during business hours. Not a widget on your site; a widget is a portal. The answers arrive where the customer asked. Anything not on the page goes to a person.
