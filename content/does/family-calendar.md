## The need
Four people, three schools or jobs, one car. Practice moved. The dentist sent a reminder by text. Grandma is arriving Thursday. Somebody has to hold all of it.

## What you used to buy
A family calendar app with a paid tier, and the habit of typing everything into it by hand. The typing was the real cost.

## Your stack does that
It reads the messages and mail where the plans actually arrive, puts them on the calendar, notices conflicts, and tells you what changed. Nobody types anything in.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the shared calendar and to the mail and messaging accounts. A scheduled task each evening that reads the day's messages for anything that looks like a plan, adds or moves it, and leaves a short note. Memory holds the standing facts: who drives on Tuesdays, which kid has which school. The first few weeks you will correct it. After that you will mostly read the note.
