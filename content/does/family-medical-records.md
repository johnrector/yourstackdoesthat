## The need
Vaccination dates for the school form. The name of the specialist from two years ago. The allergy list for the new pediatrician.

## What you used to buy
A health-records app with a subscription, or a drawer.

## Your stack does that
It reads the folder where visit summaries and portal downloads go, and answers the question when it is asked, with the date and the source.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to a folder. A skill that knows what the school form and the new-patient form ask for. Honest note: most clinic portals have no connector and will not for a while; you download the summary after each visit and drop it in the folder. The portal is a record. Keep it. The folder is your copy of the record. Keep that too.
