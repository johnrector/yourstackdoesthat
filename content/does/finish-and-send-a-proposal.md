## The need
Someone asks for a proposal. You want the work handled thoughtfully and completed without becoming its editor and coordinator.

## What you used to buy
A writing app, a proposal tool and a task list you still had to manage.

## Your stack does that
The next batch connects the request with the relationship, prior discussions and current commitments. The assistant prepares and sends the useful proposal under standing authority. Later batches assess replies and choose any follow-through. A draft returned for review is not completion unless review was requested.

## How, in plain terms
Discovery and source gathering prepare the knowledge. Deep reasoning chooses purpose and consequential scope. A finite executor composes the ordinary details, sends within actual authority and returns a receipt. The next batch is a fresh assessment, not a resumed proposal workflow. Ordinary imperfections become lessons for improving general practice.
