## The need
Three charges you do not recognize and one free trial that quietly became a bill.

## What you used to buy
A subscription-tracking app. It was, itself, a subscription.

## Your stack does that
Once a month it lists every recurring charge, flags anything new, and asks one question: which of these do you still want.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the card or bank, or the statements in a folder, and a scheduled task on the first of the month. Run each charge it finds through the [Stack Test](/stack-test/). The answers are the beginning of your own [Cancel Ledger](/ledger/).
