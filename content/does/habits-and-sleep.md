## The need
You want to know whether the thing you are trying to do is actually happening, and whether it is helping.

## What you used to buy
A habit-tracker or sleep app with a subscription and a streak you felt bad about.

## Your stack does that
It asks nothing during the day. On Sunday it writes what it knows from what you mentioned, what the calendar shows, and what the device reported if there is one, and says whether the week matched what you said you wanted.

## How, in plain terms
Memory holds the intention and the record. A scheduled task on Sunday writes a short, unsentimental note. There is no streak. If a device is involved, its data is a record that stays with the device.
