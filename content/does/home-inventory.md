## The need
If there is a fire or a flood, the insurer will ask what you had and what it was worth. Almost nobody has the list.

## What you used to buy
A home-inventory app with a subscription, or nothing, and then a very bad week.

## Your stack does that
It builds the list from your photos and receipts, keeps it current when a new receipt arrives, and produces the document the insurer wants when the insurer wants it.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the folder where photos and receipts live, and to mail for new receipts. A skill that says what the inventory should contain and how the insurer wants it formatted. Run once to build it, then a scheduled task quarterly to add what is new. The photos and receipts are the record; keep them where they are.
