## The need
Filters, gutters, the water heater, the smoke alarm batteries, the thing under the sink you were going to look at. Nothing reminds you until it fails.

## What you used to buy
A home-maintenance app with a subscription, or a notes app and good intentions.

## Your stack does that
It holds the list of what you own and when each thing was last done, and on the first of the month tells you the two or three things due, with the part number if there is one.

## How, in plain terms
Memory holds the inventory: appliances, model numbers, install dates, last service. A scheduled task on the first of the month checks the intervals and writes a short list. Nothing else is needed. The only setup is the hour it takes to tell it what you own.
