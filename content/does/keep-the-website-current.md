## The need
The hours changed. A price changed. There is a new photo. The page still says last year.

## What you used to buy
A website builder's monthly plan for the editor, or a marketing tool, or a person you paid by the hour to change a sentence.

## Your stack does that
You say the hours changed and it changes the hours, on the page, and shows you before it publishes.

## How, in plain terms
A skill for the site's voice and structure. A connector to where the site's files live, or to a host that offers one. Honest note: hosting is a relationship you keep, and a site made of plain files is the easiest kind for a stack to edit. A site that lives inside a builder's editor may need the builder's connector, and not all of them have one.
