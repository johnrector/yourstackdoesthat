## The need
Seven dinners, some constraints, one shopping trip. Somebody decides, and the deciding is the tiring part.

## What you used to buy
A meal-planning app with recipes and a synced list, paid monthly.

## Your stack does that
Every Saturday it proposes the week, using what you told it once about allergies, budget, and what got left in the fridge last time, and writes the list in the order your store is laid out.

## How, in plain terms
A skill: a page describing how your household eats, what is always in the pantry, and how you like the list sorted. A scheduled task on Saturday morning that runs the skill and leaves the plan and the list. Memory keeps what worked. Placing the order is still done through the store, by you or, where the store's stack allows it, by yours.
