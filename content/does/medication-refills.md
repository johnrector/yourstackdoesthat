## The need
Three prescriptions on different cycles, one of which runs out on a weekend.

## What you used to buy
A medication-reminder app with a subscription, or the pharmacy's app and its notifications.

## Your stack does that
It knows the cycles and says, a week ahead, which refill to request, and adds the pickup to the calendar.

## How, in plain terms
Memory holds what is taken and when it was last filled. A scheduled task each Sunday checks the cycles and leaves a note. The pharmacy holds the prescription; that is a record and a relationship with a licensed person, and it stays. This entry organizes; it does not advise. Questions about the medicine go to the pharmacist or the doctor.
