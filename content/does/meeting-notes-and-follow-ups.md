## The need
What was said, what was decided, who owes what by when, and the three emails that should go out after.

## What you used to buy
A meeting-transcription service with a per-seat subscription and a bot that joined your calls.

## Your stack does that
The next batch discovers the transcript, connects its decisions and promises with the current situation, updates useful CRM knowledge and performs the authorized follow-ups. Ordinary follow-up emails are sent, not left as unsolicited drafts.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the calendar and to the folder where recordings or transcripts land. A skill for how you want notes written. The source collector makes transcripts available to knowledge discovery. The central reasoner assesses their meaning alongside other evidence, chooses finite work and receives detailed receipts for the next batch. A transcript must actually exist and be accessible; a recording title is not its contents.
