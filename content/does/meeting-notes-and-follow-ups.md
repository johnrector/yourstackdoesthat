## The need
What was said, what was decided, who owes what by when, and the three emails that should go out after.

## What you used to buy
A meeting-transcription service with a per-seat subscription and a bot that joined your calls.

## Your stack does that
After each meeting it turns the recording or transcript into decisions and actions, drafts the follow-up emails, and adds the deadlines to the calendar.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the calendar and to the folder where recordings or transcripts land. A skill for how you want notes written. A scheduled task that runs an hour after each meeting ends. Honest note: the recording itself still has to come from somewhere, usually the meeting platform's own transcript or a device. Most stacks cannot join a call today. That is one of the rougher edges on this list.
