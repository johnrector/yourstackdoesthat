## The need
The forms, the accounts, the keys, the first-week schedule, the ten things everyone forgets to tell the new person.

## What you used to buy
Onboarding software with a per-employee price.

## Your stack does that
It runs the checklist, sends the forms, schedules the first week, and answers the new person's questions from the documents you gave it.

## How, in plain terms
A skill: the checklist and the first-week plan. A connector to the folder with the handbook and forms, and to the calendar. Honest note: payroll and benefits are records held by companies with legal obligations; keep them. The new person's own stack can read your handbook through a connector the day they start, which is the point of the [companies](/companies/) page.
