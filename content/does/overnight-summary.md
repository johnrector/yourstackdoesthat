## The need
Forty emails and a dozen messages arrived while you slept. Four need an answer. One is a problem.

## What you used to buy
An inbox-triage app or an email client's paid tier, with rules you never finished writing.

## Your stack does that
At five in the morning it reads everything that arrived, answers what has an obvious answer if you allow that, drafts replies for what does not, and leaves a two-paragraph note. You read the note in ninety seconds.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to mail and messages. A skill that says what is urgent for you, who always gets a reply, and what it may answer on its own. A scheduled task at 5:10. This is the entry that most people describe as the day their stack became theirs. Start with drafts only. Give it authority to send once you have read a few weeks of drafts.
