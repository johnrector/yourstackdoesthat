## The need
Five days somewhere. Flights, a place to stay, what to do, what to pack, how to get from the airport.

## What you used to buy
A travel-planning app with a paid tier, plus the airline's app, the hotel's app, and a notes app to hold it all.

## Your stack does that
It plans the trip against what it knows about you, holds the confirmations as they arrive, builds the day-by-day, and writes the packing list the night before.

## How, in plain terms
A skill describing how you like to travel: mornings or nights, walking or not, budget. Memory holds the constant facts, like seat preference and who needs an aisle. A connector to mail catches confirmations. Booking is still a transaction with the airline and the hotel; your stack drafts and compares, you confirm. Where a carrier's stack takes bookings directly, yours can place it under your rule.
