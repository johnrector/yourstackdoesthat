## The need
Three posts a week, on two networks, at the times people are looking.

## What you used to buy
A social media scheduler with a free tier that became a paid tier.

## Your stack does that
It drafts the week's posts from what you have written or done, holds them for your yes, and posts each at its time.

## How, in plain terms
A skill for your voice and cadence. A scheduled task that drafts on Sunday and posts through the week. A connector to each network, where one exists. Honest note: several networks do not offer a connector or restrict what one can do, and some stacks can drive a browser to post and some cannot. When it cannot post, it leaves the draft and a reminder, and you paste. That is still no subscription.
