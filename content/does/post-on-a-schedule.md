## The need
Three posts a week, on two networks, at the times people are looking.

## What you used to buy
A social media scheduler with a free tier that became a paid tier.

## Your stack does that
The batch discovers useful material, considers the audience and prepares and publishes the authorized posts at the chosen times. It requests review when that is an actual preference, rather than making review the default destination.

## How, in plain terms
A skill for your voice and cadence. The central batch reasons about the publishing commitments; finite executors publish the chosen work and return actual receipts. A connector to each network, where one exists. Account capabilities and platform restrictions differ. If publication is unavailable, the executor returns the exact gap. The next batch decides whether an authorized route exists or the principal is genuinely needed. A saved draft is a partial result, not published work.
