## The need
You have fifteen minutes with the doctor and will forget the three things you meant to ask.

## What you used to buy
A symptom-journal app with a paid tier, or nothing, and the forgetting.

## Your stack does that
Over the weeks it keeps what you told it: when the headaches came, what changed in sleep, which questions you had. The night before the visit it writes a one-page summary and the list of questions.

## How, in plain terms
Memory holds what you mentioned, in your words, with dates. A skill turns it into the one-page summary a clinician can read in a minute. Nothing here diagnoses or advises; it organizes what you noticed so the person who can advise hears all of it. The chart is the doctor's record. Keep it there.
