## The need
You want a specific thing at a specific price and do not want to check every day.

## What you used to buy
A price-tracking service or a shopping app's premium tier.

## Your stack does that
It checks the pages you named once a day and says something only when the price crosses the line you set.

## How, in plain terms
A scheduled task with a short list of pages and target prices. Some retail sites block automated reading; when that happens the task will tell you it could not check. Buying stays a decision you confirm. The stack drafts the order; you press the button.
