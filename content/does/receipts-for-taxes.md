## The need
Twelve months of receipts and statements need to become one folder and one summary by April.

## What you used to buy
A receipt-scanning app with a subscription, and a weekend in March.

## Your stack does that
It pulls receipts out of mail as they arrive, files them by category, and by January has the summary the preparer asked for.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to mail and to a folder. A skill describing what counts and how the preparer wants it organized. A scheduled task each week that files what came in. The filing itself is a relationship with the government or a preparer; that is a record, keep it. What goes away is the app in between.
