## The need
A review went up. Someone asked about you in a local group. You found out a week later.

## What you used to buy
A reputation-monitoring service with a monthly fee.

## Your stack does that
Each morning it checks the places you named, tells you what is new, and drafts a reply in your voice for anything that deserves one.

## How, in plain terms
A scheduled task with a list of pages and feeds to check. A skill for how you reply. Honest note: some platforms block automated reading or require their own login; where that is true, the task will say it could not check, and you look yourself. Replying is still yours to send.
