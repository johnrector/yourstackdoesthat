## The need
People want a slot. You want them to get one without a phone call and without double-booking.

## What you used to buy
A booking app with a monthly plan and a link you put everywhere.

## Your stack does that
The next batch considers the request alongside outgoing replies, calendar facts and other commitments. It coordinates a suitable time, makes the authorized calendar change and confirms the actual outcome. Calls and texts can acknowledge the request immediately from existing CRM knowledge.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the calendar and to the mailbox. Standing preferences and authority, an evolving CRM and a central batch reasoning cycle. Executors perform the chosen work and return receipts; the next fresh assessment decides what remains. Honest note: this works when customers write to you. If most of your customers arrive from a link and expect to click a time, a public booking page is a product you may still need, because their stacks are not booking yet. Use the [Stack Test](/stack-test/) to judge whether the actual setup carries the work without adding coordination for you.
