## The need
The school sends eleven messages a week across three channels. Two of them matter. One is about tomorrow.

## What you used to buy
A parent-communication app with notifications, and the school's own portal, and the newsletter you stopped reading.

## Your stack does that
Each evening it reads what the school sent, puts dates on the calendar, and tells you the one thing that is about tomorrow.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the mail account. A scheduled task each evening. Honest note: most school systems have no connector and post to a portal that must be opened by a person. Forward what the portal sends to mail, and let the stack read that. The portal is the school's record; leave it be.
