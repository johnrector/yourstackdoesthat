## The need
Rent, utilities, groceries, the thing one of you bought for both of you. Who owes whom, this month.

## What you used to buy
An expense-splitting app, with a paid tier for the useful parts.

## Your stack does that
It keeps a running tally from the receipts and the shared account, and at month end says who owes whom what.

## How, in plain terms
A skill for how the split works. A connector to the shared account or to a folder where receipts go. The tally is a record; keep it in a plain shared file both stacks can read. Moving the money is still done through the bank, by a person. A stack should draft the transfer and let you press the button.
