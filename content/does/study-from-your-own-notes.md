## The need
Turn a semester of notes and readings into something you can actually be tested on, at the right intervals.

## What you used to buy
A flashcard app with a subscription, and the evening spent making the cards.

## Your stack does that
It makes the questions from your notes, asks them on a spaced schedule, and keeps track of what you keep missing.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the folder with the notes and readings. A skill for how you want to be quizzed. A scheduled task that runs the review on the days the schedule says. Memory holds what you got wrong. Your notes are the record; keep them in a folder that is yours.
