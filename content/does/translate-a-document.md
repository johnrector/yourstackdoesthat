## The need
A contract in Portuguese, a customer writing in Korean, a form from a government that does not use your language.

## What you used to buy
A translation subscription, or the free version with a limit you hit in week two.

## Your stack does that
It translates the document or the thread, keeps the tone, and tells you where it is unsure.

## How, in plain terms
No setup. A skill helps if you translate the same kind of thing often and want the terms kept consistent. For a legal document, a human translator is still the record of what was agreed; the stack gets you to the point of knowing whether you need one.
