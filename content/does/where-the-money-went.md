## The need
Not a budget. A plain answer, once a month, to the question of where it went and what changed.

## What you used to buy
A budgeting app with categories you never finished setting up, paid monthly.

## Your stack does that
On the first of the month it reads the statements, sorts the spending into the handful of categories you actually think in, compares to last month, and writes two paragraphs.

## How, in plain terms
A connector to the accounts, or exported statements in a folder. A skill that names your categories and what you want to hear about. A scheduled task on the first that writes the note. No dashboard. If you want a chart, ask for one that month.
