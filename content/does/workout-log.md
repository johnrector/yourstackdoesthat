## The need
What did I lift last Tuesday, how far did I run last month, and am I actually getting anywhere.

## What you used to buy
A fitness app with a monthly fee for the history and the charts.

## Your stack does that
It keeps the log from what you tell it or from the device's data, and once a month says plainly what went up, what went down, and what you skipped.

## How, in plain terms
Memory holds the log. A skill writes the monthly note the way you want it. Honest note: the sensor data lives with the device maker, and not all of them offer a connector or an export. If yours does not, a sentence at the end of the workout is enough. The watch is a record; the app in front of it is usually a product.
