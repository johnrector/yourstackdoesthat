A new invitation / From App People to Stack People

Let your
stack
do that.

Give your attention

A personal AI can do more than answer prompts. Give it context, standing authority and a rhythm for understanding your life. It notices what needs doing and acts within your authority, without waiting for another prompt.

Meet the personal stack ↗

2009 → What comes next

Apple made “There’s an app for that” part of the language. It invited us to find a capability. “Let your stack do that” invites us to hand over the work.

01 / The shift

Who is still
doing the coordinating?

App People

You work the software.

You launch an app and follow its prescribed steps to achieve the outcome. You push the buttons, connect the steps and remember to follow up. The result is useful; getting there takes your work.

Stack People

You delegate the outcome.

You benefit from the outcome without having to launch an app or initiate the job. Your personal AI handles the steps. It understands the situation, decides the authorized work and follows through. It acts proactively instead of waiting for you to start every job. Your attention can go elsewhere.

02 / Three layers

A stack that
can grow with you.

01

Personal database

Relationships, preferences, commitments and what actually happened. The assistant administers its evolving CRM; you are not its DBA.

02

Reasoning

One executive understands the whole situation and decides what needs doing. Sophisticated reasoning gets time to do the work well.

03

Conversation

Responsive calls and texts from permitted existing knowledge. New work joins the next batch. Email follows the batch cycle entirely.

03 / Design for Batch

One fresh assessment.
A whole situation.

Replace thousands of independent continuing sessions with a batch that stands on its own.

  1. 01

    Discover knowledge

    Then gather, reconcile and prepare one connected context.

  2. 02

    Reason deeply

    Consider requests together. Save one holistic plan before acting.

  3. 03

    Do the work

    Finite jobs return detailed receipts, including failure and blockage.

↶ Next batch: receipts become knowledge. Assess the current situation afresh.

All work is batched. Calls and texts stay conversational using the prior completed batch’s permitted CRM knowledge. They accept new work without launching independent work sessions. A real deadline can start the next batch early; nothing skips the batch.

04 / The standard

Thought through.
Well done.
Done.

The proposal reaches its recipient. The appointment change is confirmed. The refund outcome is known.

A draft or a dispatch still leaves responsibility somewhere. The assistant assesses outcomes at the next batch and decides what needs attention now. You step in when it genuinely needs you.

Ordinary imperfections become lessons. The assistant improves its understanding and general approach as people and circumstances change.
