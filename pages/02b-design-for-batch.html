DFB / An operating design

Start fresh.
See the whole situation.

One fresh assessment on a timer replaces thousands of independent sessions trying to keep up. Between batches, retain knowledge: facts, relationships, commitments and receipts.

  1. 01

    Discover first

    Ask what changed, what is missing and which new sources matter. Gather actual bodies, reconcile relationships and prepare one interconnected context with coverage gaps.

  2. 02

    Reason holistically

    A capable frontier model takes the time needed. Use multiple passes when useful. Save one whole-batch plan before execution.

  3. 03

    Execute finite jobs

    The executive chooses purpose and scope. Helpers judge ordinary details. Each returns a receipt: attempt, result, evidence and remaining uncertainty.

↶ Next batch: receipts become knowledge.

Every batch stands on its own

End the executive session after its finite assignments. The next batch judges what is true now; it does not automatically resume an old plan. An unfinished commitment is a fact in the CRM, not a suspended business state. No parent session waits for a worker or a reply.

All work is batched. No exceptions.

Only calls and texts have real-time conversation. They use permitted preexisting CRM knowledge from completed batches. Missing facts and new work enter the next batch. Email is entirely batched. Urgent labels, legacy jobs and failed receipts cannot start an independent decision-making worker.

The assistant can naturally accept a cancellation and offer a reasonable notification estimate. It must not claim the change already happened. Wording fits the person and situation; no fixed acknowledgment script is required.

Discovery keeps it alive

A receipt for a new watch or note-taker can reveal a new knowledge stream. The reasoner follows the lead, investigates authorized access and records any genuine connection gap. Existing mail, calendar, incoming and outgoing texts, calls, Plaud transcripts, CRM and receipts are starting points. A fixed checklist is not discovery.

Collectors can have different schedules. Our personal Apple Messages reservoir refreshes once daily at 3 a.m. Eastern; the 3:30 batch discovers it. A later review must not claim that reservoir contains newer messages.

Done is the standard

An ordinary proposal is thought through and sent within standing authority. Drafting it or assigning it does not achieve that result. Each batch assesses outcomes and decides what needs attention now. Escalate when the principal’s judgment or new authority is genuinely needed.

The assistant administers its own evolving CRM. Save useful knowledge even when fields need to change. Learn general lessons from imperfect work and feedback. Growth matters more than tidy records; avoid named-person patches.

Choose models for their jobs

Our implementation prefers GPT-Live for conversational performance and a separate frontier model for high-effort reasoning. DFB is a builder’s choice, not a model-imposed architecture. It is also separate from OpenAI’s Batch API.

Better whole-day quality, cost and effectiveness are a thesis to measure. Human-experience targets in our design are spoken turns under 800 ms, conversational texts under 3 seconds and email around 4 hours. Those are targets, not verified universal guarantees.
