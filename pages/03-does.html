Whatever the need, this is what your stack does.

Every entry has the same four parts. The need. What you used to buy. Your stack does that. How, in plain terms, which names the part of the stack doing the work: a connector, a skill, a scheduled task, or a combination. Where setup is still real, the entry says so. Nothing here favors one frontier stack over another.

These are contributed in the open. Add one. Read them by machine at does.json or the feed.

The left-hand columns are how these needs were met in the app era and are record. The right-hand columns are the claim of this site. Every entry is possible today in a first-party stack; what has not happened yet is most people setting it up. If one of these does not work for you, that is worth knowing too. Say so.
