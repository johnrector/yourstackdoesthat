What did you stop paying for?

A public, anonymous list of subscriptions people replaced with their stack, and what replaced them. Community-contributed. Nobody is named, on either side. No vendor bashing; a product that lost to a skill file did nothing wrong. The ledger records the fact of the replacement and nothing else.

The ledger also records what the Stack Test told someone to keep. A list that only ever says "replaced" would be advertising. Lines marked kept are the test working.
