For companies

Work with
the person’s stack.

A customer’s assistant can be a useful counterpart. Give it clear records and dependable ways to do authorized work.

A stack may collect your reply in a later batch rather than keep an independent session waiting. Make the facts durable: appointment identifiers, actual changes, current options and evidence of the result.

Make outcomes legible

Distinguish a request received from a booking confirmed, a return accepted from a refund issued, and a message sent from an agreement made. Stable references help the assistant reconcile the situation without duplicate actions.

Support the relationship

Supply authorized access to useful records and actions. The person’s executive decides priorities; your interface reports what happened. Conversational acknowledgment can be fast even when the work follows a batch cadence.

This is a design recommendation, not a claim that every customer already has a working personal stack. Keep ordinary human channels available.
