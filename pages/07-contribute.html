A public working idea

Bring the lesson.
Keep the evidence.

What did your stack finish without handing the work back to you?

Contribute a capability or an experience through the public repository. Describe the need, required setup, the actual outcome and what remains rough. Examples and limitations are useful; invented results are not.

What we are learning

Design for Batch is the operating recommendation: discover knowledge, reason across the whole situation, perform finite jobs and bring receipts to the next fresh assessment. Calls and texts use permitted existing CRM knowledge in real time. All substantive work is batched.

Look for general lessons rather than a special rule for each person. Show how the assistant evolved its understanding, CRM or practices. Judge finished work and attention returned.

Read the contribution guide. Everything contributed here is public domain under CC0. Do not include private personal data or another person’s confidential communications.
